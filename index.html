Privacy Policy — Rick AI Assistant

Last updated: October 6, 2026

About this application

Rick AI Assistant is a personal AI and automation assistant. It can interact with WhatsApp and, when enabled, other connected services to help manage messages, tasks, reminders, calendar information, and personal productivity.

Information processed

Depending on the features enabled, the application may process WhatsApp messages, WhatsApp account identifiers, message metadata, tasks, reminders, calendar information, and information intentionally provided to the assistant.

Purpose of processing

Information is processed to provide the functionality of the personal assistant, including understanding requests, generating responses, managing tasks and reminders, and carrying out user-requested automations.

Service providers

The application may use third-party services necessary to provide its functionality, including Meta/WhatsApp, n8n, configured AI service providers, and other integrations explicitly enabled by the user. Information is shared with these services only where necessary for the relevant functionality.

Data sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information is shared only with service providers where necessary to operate the application, or where required by law.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the operation of the assistant and its connected services, subject to the retention practices of those services.
